# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,044 purchases across different product categories.

The main objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, product preferences, customer segments, and subscription trends.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics process using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to transform raw data into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

## 2. Dataset

- **Dataset Name:** Customer Shopping Behavior
- **Total Records:** 3,044 rows
- **Total Columns:** 18
- **Missing Values:** 37 values in the Review Rating column

### Key Features
- Customer Demographics: Age, Gender, Location
- Purchase Details: Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount
- Shopping Behavior: Discount Applied, Promo Code Used
- Customer Information: Subscription Status, Previous Purchases
- Other Features: Season, Size, Color, Review Rating, Shipping Type

## 4. Project Workflow

### Step 1: Data Loading
- Imported the dataset into Jupyter Notebook using Pandas.
- Examined the dataset structure using `df.info()`.
- Generated summary statistics using `df.describe()`.

### Step 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyzed customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Explored product categories and purchase amounts.
- Identified missing values and duplicate records.
- Examined data distributions and important features.

### Step 3: Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Handled 37 missing values in the Review Rating column using the median rating of each product category.
- Standardized column names into snake_case format.
- Created an `age_group` column by grouping customer ages.
- Created a `purchase_frequency_days` column.
- Checked redundant columns and removed `promo_code_used`.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for database analysis.

### Step 4: SQL Analysis Using PostgreSQL

Connected Python to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned dataset into the database.

Performed 10 SQL analyses to answer important business questions:

1. Revenue by Gender – Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. High-Spending Discount Users – Identified customers using discounts who spent above the average purchase amount.
3. Top 5 Products by Rating – Identified products with the highest average customer ratings.
4. Shipping Type Comparison – Compared average purchase amounts for Standard and Express shipping.
5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers – Compared customer counts, average spending, and total revenue.
6. Discount-Dependent Products – Identified products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7. Customer Segmentation – Classified customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments.
8. Top 3 Products per Category – Identified the most purchased products in each category.
9. Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions – Analyzed the relationship between repeat purchases and subscription status.
10. Revenue by Age Group – Calculated revenue contribution across different age groups.

## 6. Key Results & Insights

The analysis generated the following business insights:

### Revenue Analysis
- Male customers generated total revenue of $157,890.
- Female customers generated total revenue of $23,577.

### Subscription Analysis
- Total subscribers: 1,053.
- Total non-subscribers: 1,991.
- Average spending was $59.49 for subscribers and $59.68 for non-subscribers.

### Product Rating Analysis
Top 5 products based on average review ratings:

| Product | Average Rating |
|---|---:|
| Gloves | 3.90 |
| Sandals | 3.85 |
| Hat | 3.82 |
| Belt | 3.81 |
| T-shirt | 3.81 |

### Discount Analysis
- Hat had the highest discount rate at 68.75%.
- Sneakers followed with 61.54%.
- Pants had a discount rate of 60.90%.

### Customer Segmentation
- Loyal Customers: 2,449
- Returning Customers: 533
- New Customers: 62

## 7. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive subscriber benefits to encourage more customers to subscribe.
- **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat customers to improve customer retention.
- **Review Discount Policy:** Monitor discount-dependent products to balance promotional offers and profit margins.
- **Product Positioning:** Promote highly rated products such as Gloves and Sandals.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing campaigns on high-revenue age groups.
